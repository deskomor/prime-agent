- `/refine` now keeps outcome verdicts on past refinements: the auto-refine review (and the new `/refine review`) records whether each refinement helped, hurt, had no effect or is confounded with other edits, citing the turn that shows it. The next `/refine` sees the verdicts, a `hurt` one is named as a rollback candidate (`/refine rollback <id>`), and `autoRefine.rollbackHurt: true` rolls such local refinements back automatically before the next auto-refine.
